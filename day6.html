CSK has won five IPL titles (2010, 2011, 2018, 2021, and 2023), making it one of the most successful franchises in IPL history. For many years, the team was led by MS Dhoni, whose calm captaincy and finishing skills earned him the nickname “Thala” among fans. Under his leadership, CSK became known for its strong team spirit, experienced players, and ability to perform well in crucial matches. The team has featured many star players over the years, including Suresh Raina, Ravindra Jadeja, and Dwayne Bravo, who played important roles in its success.
